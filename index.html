    Read what happens in the demo
    1. You switch Playpen to Hybrid mode.
    2. You say: “Make me a 3D maze-chase game.”
    3. The terminal opens. Playpen’s local AI and Claude Code shake hands. Playpen saves a restore point first.
    4. They build the game together. Simpler pieces go to the local model and harder ones go to Claude Code. Each line in the terminal says which one wrote it.
    5. The voice assistant says: “Your game is ready to test.”
    6. The test pane opens and the game is playing.
    7. You ask for a new look for the main character. Your Claude usage limit is reached.
    8. You switch to Local, open the Plan tab, and plan a “redesign main character” module with the local model.
    9. Claude usage comes back. You send the module to the build, and the game plays again with a fox as the hero.
    10. You send the game to your phone.

    How it works

    You choose who does the building

    Playpen has three modes. Try each one.

    Two builders on one project

    Playpen splits a build into pieces. Simpler coding goes to the local model, in the terminal. Harder, higher-level work goes to Claude. You get more done and use less of your Claude usage.

    What’s underneath

    A desktop app
    Playpen is an Electron app that runs on your PC.
    Claude Code
    The Claude side runs through Claude Code, installed and signed in on your machine.
    A local model
    Ollama runs the local AI on your computer. Playpen picks one that fits your PC.
    Restore points
    Playpen saves your project with git before every build, so you can always go back.
    A brain per project
    Each project keeps its notes, plans and history in a folder on your computer. That is what gives every request its context.
    No Playpen servers
    Nothing runs on our servers, because we don’t have any. Your projects live on your computer.

    Beta

    We’re looking for beta testers.

    We keep your email address, whether you ticked the box, and the date. We use it to invite you to the beta and, if you ticked the box, to tell you what’s new. Email hello@playpen.site and we’ll delete it.

    Why it exists

    Playpen started with a dad trying to teach his son to make a real game, and spending a week on setup the kid would never need.

    Powerful AI tools shouldn’t be locked behind cost or setup. So Playpen is free, and it sets itself up.
