This website

No cookies, no analytics, no ad or tracking scripts. If you join the beta list we store your email address, whether you asked for updates, and the date. We use it to invite you to the beta and, if you asked, to send updates. To have it deleted, email hello@playpen.site.

The Download page asks GitHub for the latest release details from your browser. GitHub sees that request under its own privacy policy.

The Playpen app

Projects, chats and settings stay on your computer. Playpen has no servers of its own. When you choose to use Claude, your requests go to Claude, under Anthropic’s terms and privacy policy. The app doesn’t send usage analytics today.

Children

Playpen’s Child Lock lets a parent decide what can be made and when, and can keep everything on the computer. See the For parents page.
